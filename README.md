# Maternity Cover Hyderabad

A one-page comparison of 10 Indian health insurance plans for maternity, newborn/NICU and OPD cover, prepared for a couple in Hyderabad, Telangana.

**View it:** https://jagreign.github.io/maternity-compare/

Covers waiting periods, what the policy pays for delivery, newborn and NICU cover, doctor-visit (OPD) benefits, cashless hospital counts in Hyderabad and Telangana, claim settlement records and indicative premiums. Sources are listed at the bottom of the page.

This is research to help compare plans, not insurance advice. The policy wording and policy schedule are final.
