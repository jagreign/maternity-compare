Cover before or after pregnancy?

Buy before you get pregnant. Maternity cover starts only after the waiting period, counted from the day the policy starts. The delivery (hospital admission) must happen after that period ends.
Already pregnant? None of these plans will pay for that delivery. Star Women Care (and Star Super Star’s Women Care option) can still cover the baby from birth if you buy between the 12th and 20th week and Star accepts the scan reports.

Ask the insurer in writing

  1. Is the waiting period counted to the delivery date?
  2. Is the newborn covered from day 1 without a delivery claim? Up to how much, incl. NICU?
  3. Is our delivery hospital cashless for maternity?
  4. Must both spouses be on the same floater policy?
  5. What exact maternity limit is printed in our schedule?

Usually not covered

  • A pregnancy that already exists when you buy
  • Delivery before the waiting period ends
  • Cord-blood banking, baby food, diapers, non-medical items
  • C-section by choice without medical need
  • Pre/post-hospital bills around delivery in Star, Aditya Birla and Tata AIG plans

Before you pay

  • Buy soon: every month of delay pushes the first covered delivery later.
  • Declare every condition (thyroid, PCOS, BP). Non-disclosure is the top reason claims fail.
  • Tell the insurer 48–72 hours before a planned delivery for cashless approval.
  • Add the baby to the policy before 90 days.
